# Credits and provenance

## Authored fresh
Every Crew skill in this product is written from scratch. No third-party skill text, prose, code, or binaries are included or redistributed. The skills are original work.

## Methodology influences
The Crew Method draws on two open-source methodologies, credited here as influences on approach, not as sources of any shipped text:

- The discipline layer (verify before claiming done, evidence over claims, structured deterministic process, explicit guardrails) is informed by Superpowers (MIT licensed, by Jesse Vincent).
- The role-depth and workflow rhythm (a named expert role per skill, forcing questions, modes, anti-generic output) are informed by the gstack role methodology (MIT licensed, by Garry Tan).

Both are acknowledged with thanks. Neither ships in this product. The Crew skills express the Method in their own words.

## White-label
This product carries one brand: Crew. It contains no other company name, no internal team names, and no agent names. It is built to be installed and used under the purchasing business's own operating context, with their own playbooks taking authority over the defaults in each skill.

## License
The Crew skill packs are licensed to the purchasing business per the agreement under which they were supplied. The original open-source methodologies above remain under their own MIT licenses, held by their respective authors.
