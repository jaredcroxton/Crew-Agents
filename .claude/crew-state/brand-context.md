# Brand Context — PerformOS

## Identity
- Name: PerformOS
- What they do (one sentence): PerformOS builds private AI systems that give businesses leverage, not more tools to manage.
- What makes them different: Everything runs on the client's infrastructure, with their data, under their control. No cloud dependency, no vendor lock-in. Practical output, not theoretical frameworks.

## Products and services
- Primary product: CREW — 93 gold-standard AI agent skills across 14 packs
- Secondary products: LearnOS (AI training platform), AI Instruments (buy-once business tools), AI Capability Sprints (team training)
- Price points: CREW packs from single-pack to full bundle. AI Instruments are one-time purchase. Capability Sprints are per-team.
- Key features customers ask about: Private deployment, custom skill builds, team onboarding, CRM integration

## Audience
- Primary customer: Business owners and operations leaders at SMBs (10-200 employees) who want AI to do actual work, not generate more tabs
- Secondary customer: Enterprise teams who need private, on-premise AI agent systems
- What they care about: Leverage (more output, same people), privacy (their data stays theirs), practicality (things that actually ship)
- What they fear: Vendor lock-in, AI hype that wastes time, tools that create more work than they eliminate

## Voice and tone
- How they sound: Direct, confident, warm. An expert who has done the work and doesn't need to oversell it.
- Words they use: leverage, practical, private, ship, build, real, discipline, yours
- Words they never use: revolutionise, disrupt, game-changer, synergy, unlock, magic, democratise
- Reading level: Professional but plain. No jargon. No AI-industry insider language.

## Visual identity
- Primary colours (hex): #0a0a0a (ink black), #F5F5F0 (ivory)
- Accent colour (hex): #32CF32 (lime)
- Typography: Modern sans-serif, clean, architectural
- Design register: Clean and minimal
- Imagery style: Flat, dark mode, minimalist. No stock photography. No gradients. No decoration.

## Rules
- Never claims: "AI will replace your team," "set and forget," any specific ROI percentage
- Always includes: A clear path to action, an honest description of what the thing actually does
- Competitors: General AI consultancies, SaaS AI tools, agent platforms (OpenClaw, G-Stack, etc.)
- Industry regulations: Australian Privacy Act and Australian Consumer Law apply for AU-based clients
