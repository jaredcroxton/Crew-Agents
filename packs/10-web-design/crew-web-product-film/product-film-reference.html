01 / The Hide

Cut from a single hide

Every Artisan upper begins as one piece of yearling leather, wrapped whole around the last. No panels, no shortcuts, one seam up the back. It is the construction that made the boot famous, and it is still done the same way in Adelaide.

02 / The Anatomy

Every layer earns its place

Stacked leather heel, brass nails, elastic gussets, and the hide itself. Nothing decorative, nothing spare. Each component exists because the boot lasts longer with it than without it.

03 / The Standard

Nine decades of proof

From the Adelaide workshop of 1932 to sixty stores around the world, the Artisan has been resoled, restored, and handed down. A boot built once, worn for decades.
