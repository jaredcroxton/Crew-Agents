BRAND CONTEXT FILE
Brand: Shiftline, rostering and shift-cover software for commercial cleaning and facilities companies. Built inside Tidewell Facilities Group to kill the 5am call-around, then spun out in 2024 because other cleaning companies kept asking to buy it. It matters because a missed shift in cleaning is a lost contract, and most operators still roster from a spreadsheet and a group chat.
Product: Shiftline web and mobile app, rostering, shift-swap approvals, site check-in, and timesheet export. $9 per rostered worker per month, minimum $90 per month, 30 day free trial, no lock-in contract.
Customer: Operations managers and owners of cleaning and facilities companies with 20 to 500 field staff | Also influences: site supervisors who approve swaps, payroll officers who pull the timesheet export, and the cleaners themselves, who will delete any app that feels like surveillance.
Why they pick us: Built by people who actually ran cleaning crews, so the swap and cover flow matches how a 4:50am sick call really plays out | Why they leave: They outgrow us into a full workforce management suite, or a payroll export mismatch burns the payroll officer twice and trust never recovers.
Voice: A dispatcher mid-shift. Clipped, urgent, almost telegraphic. Every line opens inside a live scenario (4:50am, two callouts, one van) and lands on the fix. Verbs first. No warm-up, no wind-down, no gloss. | Always say: roster, cover, swap, crew, site, sorted | Never say: synergy, workforce optimisation, revolutionise, human capital, disrupt, empower
Always get right: The shift-swap chain. Who asked, who accepted, who approved, timestamped, every time. Customers call it the audit trail that saved them in a Fair Work query.
Where we fall short: No native payroll processing, exports only. Reporting is one fixed dashboard with no custom views. The iPad app is just the phone app scaled up.
Never claim: Never claim we guarantee award compliance or give workplace law advice. We record what happened, the customer's payroll and legal people interpret it. Never claim staff locations are tracked beyond the single site check-in moment.
Goals (6 months): 400 paying companies (from 260 today), monthly churn under 2 percent, payroll export live for the two biggest Australian payroll platforms, and 50 case-study quotes from supervisors rather than owners.
Found online: shiftline.com.au, LinkedIn (Shiftline), Capterra and Google Play reviews (4.6 average, cleaners praise the swap flow, complaints centre on the iPad layout). The Tidewell Facilities Group site links to us, our marketing never links back.
Visual identity (optional, reference only): Colours: #101418 background (near-black, the product runs light-on-dark), #E6EDF3 text, #3DDC97 accent (signal green, the colour of a covered shift) | Fonts: Archivo everywhere, headings and body, tight and utilitarian | Logo: a rounded rectangle split by a diagonal seam, dark half meeting light half, one shift handing over to the next.
Must know: Shiftline is a wholly owned subsidiary of Tidewell Facilities Group, a commercial cleaning company, spun out in 2024, and it speaks in its own voice, never the parent's. Never use Tidewell's tone, logo, or client stories in Shiftline material without a signed release. Award interpretation is sensitive, describe features, never advise. Early 2025 press wrongly labelled us employee tracking software, so tracking language is banned. Some prospects compete directly with Tidewell, and copy must never imply Tidewell can see their data.
