BRAND CONTEXT FILE
Brand: Saltbush Joinery, a six-person custom cabinetry workshop in Nambour making solid timber kitchens and built-in storage for Sunshine Coast homes. It matters because most renovators here pay custom prices for flat-pack carcasses, and Saltbush closes that gap with true made-to-measure work you can watch being built.
Product: Custom kitchens, designed, built, and installed, $28,000 to $65,000 per kitchen. Wardrobes and laundry fit-outs from $6,500.
Customer: Owner-renovators aged 35 to 60 within an hour of the workshop, usually a couple deciding together | Also influences: builders and interior designers who refer jobs, and the site builder who controls the install window.
Why they pick us: They can walk the workshop, meet the tradesperson building their kitchen, and get one fixed quote that does not move unless the scope does | Why they leave: our 10 to 14 week lead time, when a kitchen showroom promises four weeks from a catalogue.
Voice: The senior tradesperson at the bench, unhurried and warm, happy to let a sentence run long when the detail deserves it, lingers on how things feel in the hand (a drawer should sigh shut, an edge should read soft under your thumb before you ever look at it) | Always say: made to measure, solid timber, fixed quote, the workshop, walk you through it | Never say: bespoke luxury, cheap, discount, premium lifestyle, world-class, trust us.
Always get right: measurements and dates. When we quote an install date, we hit it, and clients mention that in almost every review.
Where we fall short: email replies go quiet during install weeks, we have no showroom, and the website gallery is two years out of date.
Never claim: that we are the cheapest option, or that solid timber will never move or mark. It is a natural material and we say so up front.
Goals (6 months): 22 signed kitchen contracts, average job value lifted from $38,000 to $42,000, the wardrobe range launched as a standalone offer, and Google reviews grown from 31 to 60 without buying a single one.
Found online: saltbushjoinery.com.au, Instagram @saltbushjoinery, a Facebook page used mostly for job photos, Google Business Profile (4.9 stars, 31 reviews), and a Houzz portfolio nobody has updated since the gallery went stale.
Visual identity (optional, reference only): Colours: #2F4538 primary (deep eucalyptus green), #C8894B accent (spotted gum tan), #F7F4EC background (limewash off-white) | Fonts: Fraunces for headings, Inter for body | Logo: a single saltbush leaf set inside a square dovetail-joint outline, wordmark underneath in Fraunces.
Must know: QBCC licence number 15098765 must appear on every quote, ad, and the website footer (Queensland law). All timber is FSC-certified and clients ask about it, so keep the certification claim exact. A local competitor collapsed in 2025 leaving unfinished kitchens across the coast, some of our clients are those victims, never joke about it and never name the company. The workshop shuts December 19 to January 12, do not promise install dates inside that window.
