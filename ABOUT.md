# About CREW

CREW is built by [PerformOS](https://performos.com.au).

PerformOS builds private AI systems for business. We do not chase tools. We build leverage.

Founded by Jared Croxton, PerformOS creates practical AI agents, business tools, and training programmes that help teams automate work, make better decisions, and ship faster. Everything we build runs on your infrastructure, with your data, under your control.

**What CREW is built on:**

- **Private by default.** Skills run on your machine. Your business data never leaves your environment. No cloud dependency, no vendor lock-in.
- **Leverage, not more tools.** CREW does not give you another dashboard to check. It gives your existing AI agents the skills to actually do the work.
- **Practical, not theoretical.** Every skill was built from real business requirements: lead research, FAQ building, invoice workflows, performance conversations. No filler.
